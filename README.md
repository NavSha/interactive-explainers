# AI for Builders — Interactive Explainers

Interactive guides explaining modern AI concepts — built for people shipping AI products who need to understand what's actually going on under the hood.

**[Read the explainers →](https://navsha.github.io/interactive-explainers/)**

## Topics

| Series | Parts | What you'll learn |
|--------|:-----:|-------------------|
| **How LLMs Actually Work** | 3 | Training, inference, and where models break down |
| **Context Windows & Memory** | 3 | Token limits, conversation design, and retrieval patterns |
| **Cost & Latency Tradeoffs** | 3 | Pricing, optimization levers, and when bigger models aren't better |
| **Grounding & Guardrails** | 4 | RAG, retrieval quality, and safety layers |
| **Evaluation & Testing** | 4 | Evals, metrics, and shipping AI you can trust |
| **Agents & Tool Use** | 4 | The agent loop, tool calling, and orchestration patterns |
| **AI UX & Human-in-the-Loop** | 4 | Uncertainty UX, review queues, and oversight architectures |
| **The Builder's Guide to Vibe Coding** | 4 | Building with AI-assisted coding tools as a non-engineer |
