## Summary

Every AI feature lives inside a triangle of cost, quality, and latency — improving one axis typically worsens another. The site calls this the closest thing to a universal framework for AI product decisions. Bigger models cost more but hallucinate less; agent loops improve quality but multiply spend and latency; streaming hacks perception but doesn't reduce compute.

## PM takeaway

Never evaluate an AI feature on one axis. Every proposal should answer: what does this cost per call, how fast is it, and how good is it — and which axis are we willing to sacrifice?
