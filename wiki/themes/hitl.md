## Summary

HITL is the pattern where a human reviews, corrects, or approves AI output before it takes effect. It's the practical answer to the reliability gap — when models aren't good enough to fully automate, you keep a human in the critical path. The site treats HITL as both a product pattern (review queues, escalation) and an evaluation mechanism (human feedback as ground truth).

## Builder takeaway

HITL is a spectrum, not a binary. The key builder decision is where on the automation–oversight continuum each workflow sits, and how that shifts as model quality improves.
