## Summary

The site's core thesis: everything LLMs do reduces to predicting the most likely next token. This single mechanism produces language understanding, reasoning, code generation, and — critically — hallucination. Understanding next-token prediction is the prerequisite for understanding why LLMs behave the way they do.

## PM takeaway

When an LLM surprises you — brilliant answer or confident nonsense — ask: "what would the most probable next token be?" That mental model explains both the magic and the failure modes.
